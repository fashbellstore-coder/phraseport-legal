Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

PhrasePort ("the app") is published by Hussain Nasir Khan ("we"). Contact: hussainnasirkhan@icloud.com.

Summary

See below for exactly what is collected and why.

What stays on your device

Settings and progress are stored only on your device, using the operating system's standard app storage. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The app offers optional paid features. Payments are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.

To confirm what you have bought and to let you restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat (revenuecat.com). RevenueCat receives your purchase history and an anonymous app user ID generated by the service, together with basic device and app information (such as device type, OS version and country) and your IP address. This data is used only to verify entitlements and operate your purchase. It is not linked to your name or email and is not used for tracking or advertising. RevenueCat's policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Your choices

To remove local data, delete the app. PhrasePort has no subscriptions; the one-time purchase does not renew.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above.
